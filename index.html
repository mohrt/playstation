PlayStation BIOS

The emulator starts with OpenBIOS, the free BIOS written by the PCSX-Redux team, so no BIOS of your own is needed. Some games need Sony's BIOS: if one doesn't start, choose or drop your own console's BIOS image file. Sony's BIOS is still under copyright, so none is included here. Yours stays in this browser and is never sent anywhere. The browser remembers it, so you only need to do this once.

Your PlayStation BIOS Optional

The BIOS ROM from your own console. Exactly 512 KB (524,288 bytes). Known dumps are named by console model (SCPH-1001 and so on); a known damaged dump of the SCPH-5502 is refused (it crashes before the Sony logo).

Often named scph1001.bin, scph5501.bin or scph7502.bin.

The retrobios collection keeps Sony's BIOSes in its PlayStation folder, named by model: scph1001.bin (USA), scph1002.bin (Europe) and scph1000.bin (Japan).

Release to insert

Click or press a key to start sound

Keyboard (player 1): arrow keys for the D-pad, S, Z, X and C for triangle, square, cross and circle, Q and E for L1 and R1, 1 and 3 for L2 and R2, Enter for Start and right Shift for Select. Hold Tab or Space to fast-forward. Gamepads work as soon as you press a button; the first is player 1, the second player 2, laid out as on a DualShock (sticks included, with the DualShock controller and its Analog button on).
